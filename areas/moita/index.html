What it costs

On-site visits and pick-up and return are €20 an hour, and remote sessions are €15 an hour. Common jobs have a fixed price, such as €35 for virus removal, €40 for a Windows reinstall and €30 for an SSD upgrade. A diagnosis is €15, and it's free if you go ahead with the repair. Travel to Moita is a flat €5. You pay once the job is done, by MB WAY or cash. The prices page has the full list.

When I'm available

How it works

  1. Tell me what's wrong. Call, message me on WhatsApp or send an email. A couple of sentences is enough.

  3. You know the price first. I tell you the cost before I start, and nothing gets added without asking you.
