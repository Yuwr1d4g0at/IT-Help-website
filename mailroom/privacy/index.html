Mailroom privacy policy

Mailroom is a personal Gmail dashboard I run for my own accounts. This page explains what it does with the Google data it can see.

Updated November 2026

What Mailroom accesses

When a Gmail account is connected, Mailroom can:

  • read messages and attachments, to show them on the dashboard;
  • change the Inbox, Unread and Starred labels, to archive, mark read or unread, and star messages;
  • send email that I write in Mailroom.

It never deletes email.

What is stored

Only the Google sign-in tokens, encrypted with AES-256-GCM on my home server. Email content is fetched from Gmail when it's shown and is not stored. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

Where it runs

Mailroom runs on my own server at home and is reachable only from my own devices over a private network. No other company hosts it.

Removing access

Disconnect the account in Mailroom, or remove Mailroom at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Either one ends its access immediately and the stored token is deleted.

Google API data

Mailroom's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
