Mailroom

Mailroom is a private Gmail dashboard I built for my own inboxes. It runs on a small server at home and only my own devices can open it.

What it does

  • Shows my Gmail inboxes side by side, with unread counts and the newest messages.
  • Lets me read, search, archive, star and reply to email in one place.
  • Shows attachments and previews images and PDFs.

Who can use it

Only me. Mailroom isn't offered to the public and you can't sign up for it. It connects only to Gmail accounts I own.

Privacy

Mailroom never deletes email and doesn't store or share message content. Read the Mailroom privacy policy for the details.
